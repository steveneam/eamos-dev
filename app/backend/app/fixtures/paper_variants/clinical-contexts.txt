Results
The RPE65 proband carried NM_000329.3:c.260A>G (p.Asp87Gly).
The variant segregated with disease in the family.

Experimental validation
Site-directed mutagenesis produced an RPE65 p.His313Ala construct.
Wild-type RPE65 was compared with p.His313Ala.
